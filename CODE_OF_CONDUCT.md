# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in TokenLens welcoming, respectful,
and safe for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept actionable feedback, and
focus criticism on ideas and behaviour rather than people. Do not share
credentials, customer telemetry, prompts, responses, identifiers, or
contractual pricing in issues, pull requests, or public discussions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private or sensitive
information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact in
`SECURITY.md`. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report. Maintainers
will review reports, protect confidentiality where possible, and may remove
content, restrict participation, or ban accounts. Appeals should be sent
through the same private channel and reviewed by a maintainer not involved in
the original decision.

This code applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the
project publicly.
